# Vantelyx CLM — University of Utah Positioning

> Use this for today's written submission. Lead with fit and outcomes, not feature count.
> Do not try to out-feature Ironclad/Icertis on their terms — win on the terms that matter to a public research university.

## One-line positioning

Vantelyx is a contract **operations** platform purpose-configured for higher education and
public-sector procurement — combining an executive-friendly command center, decision-support
AI that keeps your data in your tenant, and audit-ready governance, deployed in weeks not quarters.

## The three messages to repeat everywhere

1. **Built for how universities actually work** — departments, sponsored research, public-records
   obligations, and non-lawyer users — not retrofitted enterprise sales software.
2. **AI you can trust and govern** — decision-support, never autopilot; runs against your own
   data, with a clear path to private/on-prem models so contracts never leave your control.
3. **Fast, adaptable, and affordable** — a single-institution deployment in weeks, configurable
   by your team, without a multi-quarter implementation contract.

## Why us over the incumbents (the honest framing)

| Buyer concern | Ironclad / Icertis / DocuSign CLM | Vantelyx |
|---|---|---|
| Time-to-value | 6–12 month implementations | Weeks; demo-ready today |
| Usability for non-legal staff | Legal-centric, steep | Executive/department-friendly, next-action UX |
| Higher-ed & public-sector fit | Generic; add-on at best | Purpose-built: research contracts, records/retention, RFP trails |
| AI data governance | Cloud AI, vendor-controlled | Decision-support, your-tenant data, private-LLM roadmap |
| Cost & flexibility | Premium, rigid SOW | Lower TCO, configurable in-house |
| Public-records / FOIA / retention | Afterthought | First-class governance design |

> **Do not claim** depth you don't have (millions-of-contracts clause AI, SOC2 today, dozens of
> native integrations). Claim **fit, speed, governance, and adaptability** — all true and all
> where incumbents are weak for this buyer.

## Differentiators that are real and demoable

- **Command Center** — every contract has owner, status, next action, risk, renewal clock, obligations.
- **Vantelyx Copilot** — plain-language Q&A over contracts (high-risk, renewals, approvals,
  obligations, vendor issues), with confidence levels and human-review reminders. No hallucinated
  certainty — a deliberate, defensible design for legal users.
- **Natural-language repository search** — "high-risk vendor agreements expiring in 90 days."
- **Obligations as accountable tasks**, renewal risk scoring (not just calendar reminders),
  clause deviation explained in business language.
- **Role-based access** across 7 roles with value-based approval authority.
- **Audit-ready activity trail** and exportable registers (contracts, obligations, renewals, audit log).

## Higher-ed wedges to emphasize (incumbents don't lead with these)

- **Sponsored research & grants**: research agreements, sub-awards, F&A/indirect-cost terms,
  industry NDAs, material transfer agreements.
- **Public-records readiness**: open-records/FOIA export, retention schedules, legal hold.
- **Procurement governance**: RFP/sole-source documentation, board-approval trails, the built-in
  RFP coverage matrix for evaluators.
- **Data sovereignty**: AI runs on your data; roadmap to Azure OpenAI / on-prem (Ollama) so
  nothing leaves university control.

## Proof-points language (safe, accurate)

- "Decision-support AI, reviewed by authorized business/legal users before action."
- "Architected for SSO/SAML, multi-tenant isolation, encryption, and immutable audit; security
  hardening is sequenced ahead of production go-live."
- "Configurable to the University's departments, contract types, approval policies, and reporting."

## What to explicitly position as roadmap (sets honest expectations, avoids POC traps)

Document ingestion/OCR at scale, e-signature integration, ERP/SIS integration (Banner/Workday/
PeopleSoft), and security certifications (SOC2) are on a sequenced roadmap — framed as
"purpose-built and adaptable," not "missing."
